Whistle

A double pendulum carrying a whistle, heard through two microphones in a reverberant square domain.

Drag the two microphones anywhere in the domain. Keys: Space pause · R randomize. Sound always travels at 343 m/s in real time, so the time-scale slider also sets how fast the whistle moves relative to sound — that is, how much Doppler you get.
